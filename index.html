Is it worth it? Value of living in 30 US metros

Each metro scores 1–100 for an early-career finance professional, weighing what a place costs against what it gives back. Change the weights to match your priorities.

Cheap or expensive, how much does the city give back?

Scores are relative standings across these 30 metros (10 most populous in each home-price tier), not absolute measures; gaps of a few points are not meaningful. Data: Zillow (Sep 2026), BEA price parities (2024), BLS wages (May 2025), ACS 2025, County Health Rankings 2025, NOAA 1991–2020 normals. Entry-level pay is proxied by the 25th-percentile business and finance wage. Health, safety and social measures are county data mapped to metros. Weather for New York uses Newark; Riverside’s is from a secondary source. Wage columns exclude bonuses.
